# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing others' private
information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the
[repository's private reporting form](https://github.com/skbariz/prediction-market-arbitrage/security/advisories/new)
(mark the report "Code of Conduct"), or through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
